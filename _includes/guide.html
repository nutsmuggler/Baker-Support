Step by step

One instruction at a time, read aloud when your hands are full

Start a bake and Knead drops the recipe away, leaving the step you're on: what to do, how long it takes, and nothing else to scroll past with floury fingers.

Ask out loud, listen from across the kitchen

Tap the waveform in the corner and the session takes voice: "read that again", "how long", "next step". Knead speaks the instruction back, so you can keep both hands in the dough.

Where you are, always in view

The header carries the step count and the time this step still needs — 2/3, about 12 hours — and Set Alarm underneath is already loaded with that exact duration.

Leave and come back

Close the session and the bake stays where you left it. The list icon at the bottom opens the whole method if you'd rather read ahead, then returns you to the current step.
