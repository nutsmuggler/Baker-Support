The Diary

Every loaf remembers what you did differently

Baking the same recipe twice never gives you the same bread. The Diary is where you write down why — a note per bake, attached to the recipe it came from.

A note, dated and attached

Finish a bake and Knead offers you an empty page stamped with the date. Write as much or as little as you like — the recipe it belongs to is already clipped to the entry.

Read the last bake before the next one

Open any recipe and its past entries sit underneath it, newest first. Two hours more rest, a hotter oven, less water in August — the correction is there when you start again.

Yours, and only yours

Entries stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud account, so the Diary follows you from iPhone to iPad without passing through us.
