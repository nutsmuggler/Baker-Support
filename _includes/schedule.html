Move the start, watch the day move

Change the start date or time on the schedule and every step slides with it, right through to Ends at. Tap Expand all to read the full instruction behind each timing.

Grouped by Today and Tomorrow

Long bakes cross midnight, so the schedule is split by day rather than by hour count. A 2:34 am fold is easier to notice — and to plan away from — when it's sitting under Tomorrow.

Alarms carry the waiting

During a bake, each timed step offers Set Alarm for exactly its duration. If a reminder never sounded, allow notifications for Knead in Settings — that's the culprit nine times out of ten, not the starter.
